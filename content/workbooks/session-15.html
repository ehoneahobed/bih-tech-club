Station 2: Bookmark & Download
Session 15 • Page 3

1. Your Task

Save a web page as a bookmark, download a file, put it in the right folder with the right name, and find it again. Time limit: 10 minutes.

🧰 YOU NEED

A PC with internet and a browser; the web page and the file your mentor names on the board (for example the club's Session 15 page and its workbook PDF).

📁 THE RIGHT PLACE

Folder: Documents > BIH_TechClub > Online_Basics (make Online_Basics if it is not there). File name: TechClub_SkillsLab2_YourName, e.g. TechClub_SkillsLab2_AmaMensah.pdf.

1
Go direct: type the page's address into the address bar (not into a search engine).
2
Bookmark it on the bookmarks bar with the name Skills Lab 2.
3
Download the file your mentor names. Find it in the browser's downloads list.
4
Tidy it: in File Explorer, move it from Downloads to the Online_Basics folder and rename it with the naming standard.
5
Prove it: close everything. Then reopen the page from your bookmark, and find your file again using the search box in File Explorer. Show your mentor.
💡 HINTS FOR A RE-TRY
Bookmark: Ctrl+D, then choose the bookmarks bar (Edge: Favourites bar). Show the bar: Ctrl+Shift+B. Downloads list: Ctrl+J. File Explorer: Win+E, then Downloads in the left pane. Move: cut (Ctrl+X) and paste (Ctrl+V). Rename: F2.

Station 3: Email with an Attachment
Session 15 • Page 4

1. Your Task

Send your mentor a correctly formatted email that attaches the file from Station 2. Use the Session 13 rules. Time limit: 10 minutes.

🧰 YOU NEED

The Hub email account your mentor gives you; the mentor's address from the board; your file TechClub_SkillsLab2_YourName.pdf (from Station 2). If you have not done Station 2 yet, your mentor will tell you which file to attach.

📧 THE EMAIL

To: your mentor's address. Subject: Skills Lab 2 - JHS 1A - Your Name (use your own class and name). Body: the 4 parts: greeting, polite opening, 2–3 sentences saying what you attached, sign-off with full name and class.

Draft your email body here first:

Dear ...,
I hope ...
I have attached ...
Yours sincerely, ...
💡 HINTS FOR A RE-TRY
No spaces in the address: check every letter. The subject line is 4–7 words, no CAPITALS shouting and no "!!!". Attach with the paperclip icon, then go to Documents > BIH_TechClub > Online_Basics. Wait until the file has finished uploading before pressing Send. Read the whole email once more before you send it.
🚪 BEFORE YOU LEAVE THE STATION
Sign out of the email account, close the browser, and never let the browser save the password on this shared PC.

Time taken:

... minutes

2. Mentor Checklist

Email arrived in the mentor's inbox (address spelt correctly).
Clear subject line with topic, class and name (4–7 words).
Body has all 4 parts: greeting, opening, short message, sign-off with full name and class.
The correct file is attached and opens (a safe type: .pdf or .docx).
Polite netiquette: no ALL CAPS, no text-speak.
Pupil signed out and closed the browser. Finished within 10 minutes.
