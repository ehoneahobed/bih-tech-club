Session 1: Meet Your Computer

Welcome! Whether you are sitting in front of a Desktop computer or a Laptop, this workbook will guide you step-by-step. You do not need any prior experience.

1. What is a Computer? (The Corn Mill Analogy)

Think about a local neighborhood corn mill:

  • 1. INPUT: You pour raw, dry corn into the funnel hopper.
  • 2. PROCESS: The motor grinds and crushes the grains inside the machine housing.
  • 3. OUTPUT: Clean, fine corn meal pours into your waiting basin.

Every computer—desktop or laptop—works the exact same way:

Figure 1.0: The flow of computing. Input becomes Output through internal processing.

2. Which Machine Are You Using Today?

🖥️ OPTION A: Desktop Computer

A stationary machine designed to stay on a table. It is made of separate pieces connected with cables: a monitor screen, a system unit tower, an external keyboard, and a mouse.

💻 OPTION B: Laptop Computer

A portable all-in-one computer. The screen folds down over the keyboard. It has an internal rechargeable battery, a built-in keyboard, and a flat touchpad that acts as the mouse.

3. Hardware vs. Software in 30 Seconds

Hardware (The Physical Body) Software (The Brain's Programs)
Anything you can physically touch with your hand. The invisible instructions that tell the machine what to do.
Examples: Screen glass, keys, mouse, touchpad, battery. Examples: Word processor, Scratch coding, games, web browser.

3. Critical Care Rules for Laptops

⚠️ THREE GOLDEN LAPTOP CARE RULES
  1. Open from the center: Always open the laptop lid by lifting gently from the center of the rim. Never yank it open from just one corner, or the hinge may crack!
  2. Two hands at all times: When carrying a laptop, close the screen completely and carry it with two hands close to your chest like a valuable textbook. Never hold it by its screen!
  3. Keep vents clear: Never place a running laptop on a soft mattress, pillow, or cloth. It breathes air through vents on the bottom. Always use a flat, hard desk so it does not overheat.
Safe Startup: Desktop vs. Laptop
Session 1 • Page 3

1. Choose Your Machine's Startup Pathway

Follow the column that matches the machine you are sitting in front of today:

🖥️ If You Have a Desktop:
1
Wall Switch & UPS: Check that the wall switch and the black UPS box are ON (green light). Your mentor switches these on.
2
System Unit Power: Press the large circular power button [ (I) ] on the front of the System Unit once. Listen for the fan breeze.
3
Monitor Power: Look at the bottom-right rim of the monitor. If the small power light is off, press the monitor power button once.
4
Wait to Boot: Keep your hands off. Wait 30–60 seconds for the Desktop screen to load.
💻 If You Have a Laptop:
1
Open the Lid Gently: Hold the base down with one hand. Use your other hand to lift the lid from the center until it forms an open angle.
2
Check Power Cord: If the battery is low, ensure the charging adapter cable is plugged into the small side charging port and wall socket.
3
Find Laptop Power Key: Look at the top-right corner of the built-in keyboard (or the side edge). Press the small power button once firmly.
4
Wait to Boot: Do not touch any keys. Wait for the screen to light up and show the Welcome screen.

3. Startup Troubleshooting ("What Went Wrong?")

🚨 MY MACHINE IS NOT TURNING ON!
  • If using a Desktop: Check the monitor power light first. If the System Unit fan is dead silent, check whether the UPS or wall socket is switched ON.
  • If using a Laptop: The battery might be completely flat ("dead"). Plug in the charging cord and look for a tiny amber/white charging LED on the side edge. Wait 2 minutes, then press the power button again.
Pointer Control: Mouse vs. Touchpad
Session 1 • Page 4

1. Controlling the Pointer (Two Ways)

To control the arrow on screen, Desktop users use a Mouse, while Laptop users use a built-in Touchpad (also called a Trackpad). Both achieve the exact same results!

Figure 1.3: Mouse buttons vs. Touchpad click zones. Notice the left-right color match!

2. Master Action Reference Guide

Action 🖥️ How to do it with a MOUSE 💻 How to do it on a TOUCHPAD
Move Pointer Slide the mouse across the pad. Glide one finger lightly across the touchpad.
Left-Click (Select) Tap the Left Button once with your index finger. Click the bottom-left corner firmly, OR gently tap the pad surface once.
Double-Click (Open) Tap the Left Button twice fast: Click-Click! Tap your finger twice quickly on the pad surface.
Right-Click (Menu) Tap the Right Button with your middle finger. Click the bottom-right corner firmly with one finger.
Scroll Page Roll the center plastic wheel forward/back. Place two fingers together and slide them up or down simultaneously.

3. Screen Cursor Shapes

Look closely at your screen: The pointer changes shape to tell you what it is doing!
• White Arrow: Normal state, ready to point and select.
• Hand with Pointing Finger: You are hovering over a website link or clickable button.
• "I" Bar (I-Beam): You are over a text area; click here to start typing words.
• Spinning Blue Circle: Hold on! The computer is busy thinking. Do not click.
Hands-On Practice & Safe Shutdown
Session 1 • Page 5

1. Guided Practical Lab: Your First Screen Actions

Complete these 3 foundational challenges at your workstation:

A
Challenge 1: The Screen Orbit
Mouse Users: Slide the mouse until the arrow reaches all 4 corners of your screen. If you run out of desk space, lift the mouse in the air, put it back in the middle of the pad, and glide again.
Touchpad Users: Practice gliding one index finger to each corner. If you reach the edge of the touchpad, lift your finger, place it in the center, and glide again!
B
Challenge 2: Icon Selection Practice
Move your arrow over the Recycle Bin icon on the desktop.
Perform a single Left-Click (or tap once on your touchpad).
Observation: Notice how the icon is highlighted in blue. Now click any empty space on the wallpaper to deselect it.
C
Challenge 3: Reveal the Secret Options Menu
Move the pointer to an empty space on the desktop. Perform a Right-Click (Right mouse button OR bottom-right touchpad corner).
Observation: A menu pops up! Tap the [Esc] key on your keyboard to close it safely.
