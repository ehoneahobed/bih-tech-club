2. The "Keep It Manageable" Rule

⚠️ DO NOT TRY TO BUILD FACEBOOK IN 3 DAYS!
The most common mistake young innovators make is choosing an impossible project (e.g. "We want to build a self-driving car in Scratch").

The Pro Rule: Choose a clear, simple problem that you can build, polish, and demonstrate beautifully in 3 sessions. One finished, bug-free spreadsheet or 3-slide presentation beats a broken, half-finished giant dream!
What is a Prototype? (Version 0.1)
Session 20 • Page 3

1. Plain Talk: The Cardboard Model House

An architect never pours concrete on day one. First, they cut cardboard and tape together a small scale model of the building. It has no real glass windows or working light switches, but it shows everyone the size, shape, and door positions.

In tech, this early working draft is called a Prototype (Version 0.1). It is built quickly so you can test if your concept makes sense before spending hours on final styling!

Figure 20.1: The Capstone Evolution. Today we lock in the Blueprint and start the raw Prototype.

2. Safe Data Ethics for Capstone

🔒 CAPSTONE DATA PROTECTION LAW
Do not collect real telephone numbers, personal home addresses, or secret financial records from real people in your neighborhood!

Use Realistic Simulated Data: If building a canteen spreadsheet, use sample items (Jollof Rice, Meat Pie, Sobolo) and approximate prices. Protect community privacy at all times!

Guided Hands-On Lab: Setting the Foundation
Session 20 • Page 5

1. Lab Mission: Set Up Folders & Build Prototype v0.1

Follow these 5 steps to organize your files and begin your project skeleton:

1
Build Your Capstone Directory Tree:
Press ⊞ Win + E → Navigate to Documents > BIH_Practice.
Create a main folder: Capstone_[TeamName].
Inside it, create two subfolders: Drafts and Final_Showcase.
2
Launch Your Chosen Software Tool:
• Track A: Open Word (notice); PowerPoint later (3-slide pitch).
• Track B: Open Excel / Calc → Create blank workbook.
• Track C: Open Scratch → Start fresh project.
• Track D: Open Word (report); PowerPoint later (photo/slide guide).
3
Build the "Skeleton" Structure (Version 0.1):
Do not worry about colors or fancy graphics today! Build the structure:
• Word users: Type the main Title and 3 section headings.
• Excel users: Type Column Headers across Row 1 (Item, Qty, Price, Total).
• PowerPoint users: Create your 3 slides and type the titles on each.
• Scratch users: Choose your main Sprite and Backdrop.
4
Initial Save (Save As...):
Save your file into Documents > BIH_Practice > Capstone_[TeamName] > Drafts.
Use the naming formula: Capstone_v0.1_[ProjectName].docx (or .xlsx / .pptx / .sb3).
5
Mentor Blueprint Inspection:
Raise your hand and show your Canvas (Page 4) and your Version 0.1 Skeleton to your mentor for sign-off!
