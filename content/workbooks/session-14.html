Session 14: Filling Forms Online

Very soon, important things in your life will start with an online form: choosing your senior high school, applying for a scholarship, registering for a competition or a holiday camp. One wrong letter or a missing file can cause real trouble. Today you will learn to fill in any online form accurately, safely and calmly, and to keep proof that you sent it.

1. Plain Talk: The Very Strict Registration Clerk

Think of registering at a clinic. The nurse at the desk asks your name, your date of birth and a phone number, and writes them on a card. If you say "Akos", she may ask, "Is that Akosua?" She can notice a mistake and help you fix it.

An online form is a registration desk with a very strict clerk: the computer. It cannot guess what you meant. It saves exactly what you type, spelling mistakes and all, and that information may later be printed on your admission letter or certificate. So the person filling in the form must be the careful one.

Know Your Form Fields

1. The Building Blocks of Every Form

Each place where you give an answer is called a field. Forms use a few kinds of field again and again:

Figure 14.1: A practice form showing the eight things you will meet on almost every online form.
⭐ THE RED STAR MEANS "REQUIRED"
A field marked with an asterisk * must be filled in. If you leave it empty, the form will not send and usually shows a red message such as "This is a required question". Fill it in and press Submit again.

2. Tips for Each Field

FieldTip

Radio vs checkboxRound radio buttons allow only one choice. Square checkboxes allow several (or one "I agree" tick).

Getting Every Detail Exactly Right
Session 14 • Page 3

1. Names: Exactly as on Your Documents

Type your name exactly as it is on your birth certificate and school records: same spelling, same order, no nicknames. If one form says "Akosua Boateng" and another says "Akos Boatemaa", an office may think they are two different people. Forms often split the name into parts:

Surname (family name)First nameOther names (middle names)
BoatengAkosuaSerwaa

Start each name with a capital letter, then small letters, unless the form asks for CAPITALS. If Caps Lock is on by mistake, everything comes out in capitals: check the light on the keyboard.

2. Dates, Phone Numbers and Emails

📅 DATES

In Ghana we write the day first: 04/07/2012 means 4 July 2012. Some forms (often American ones) want the month first, so the same numbers would mean 7 April! Always read the hint, such as DD/MM/YYYY.

📞 PHONE NUMBERS

A Ghana mobile number has 10 digits and starts with 0, e.g. 024 xxx xxxx. With the country code you drop the 0: +233 24 xxx xxxx. Many forms want it typed without spaces: 024xxxxxxx. Only give a parent's number, with permission.

📧 EMAILS

No spaces, every letter right (Session 13). Use the address your parent or school gives you. A wrong email means you never receive the confirmation.

⚠️ WATCH OUT FOR AUTOFILL ON SHARED COMPUTERS
Browsers remember what was typed before and offer to autofill it. On a shared computer it may fill in someone else's name or phone number. Check every field the browser fills for you, and never let a shared computer save your details.

Uploads, Submitting & Staying Safe
Session 14 • Page 4

1. Uploading a Document or Photo

📏 READ THE RULES FIRST

Format: photos are usually JPG or PNG; documents are usually PDF. Size: e.g. "max 1 MB" (remember KB and MB from Session 6). Photo: passport style, plain light background, face straight, no filters. Give the file a clear name, e.g. Photo_AkosuaBoateng.jpg.

📱 NO SCANNER? USE A PHONE

Android: Google Drive app > + > Scan. iPhone: Notes app > camera icon > Scan Documents. Both make a PDF. Lay the paper flat in good light with all four corners showing. File too big? Ask your mentor to help make a smaller copy.

2. Before and After You Press Submit

✅ BEFORE: THE FINAL CHECK

1. Every * field is filled. 2. Names match your documents. 3. Dates are in the right format and the year is right. 4. The right file is attached. 5. A trusted adult has checked a real form. Then press Submit once.

🧾 AFTER: KEEP THE PROOF

Write down the reference number. Then save the confirmation page: Ctrl+P > Save as PDF (or print it), or take a screenshot with Win+Shift+S. Look for a confirmation email too.

3. When the Form Goes Wrong

What happensWhat to do
A red message under a fieldRead it: it tells you what is wrong ("Enter a valid phone number"). Fix that field and submit again.
"Session expired" or "timed out"You took too long and the page forgot your answers. Start again. Next time get all your details and files ready before you start.
The page freezes or shows an error after SubmitDo not press Submit again and again. Wait, then check for a confirmation email or reference. If there is none, try once more later, or ask an adult.
The website is very slowMany people may be using it near a deadline. Try at a quieter time, and never leave a form until the last day.
🛡️ STAYING SAFE WITH REAL FORMS
1. Only use the official website given by your school or an official letter. Check the address (for example .gov.gh) and the padlock. 2. Never pay a stranger who promises to "help" or to "guarantee" a placement or scholarship. 3. Never share a PIN, password or voucher code with anyone except the trusted adult helping you. 4. Never fill in forms from links in WhatsApp messages or pop-ups. 5. Always ask a trusted adult (parent, guardian or teacher) before you submit a real form.
