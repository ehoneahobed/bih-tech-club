Session 10: Skills Lab 1: Know Your Machine

Today there is nothing new to learn. Instead, you show what you can already do. A Skills Lab is like the practical part of an exam, but friendlier: you work on a real computer, the mentor watches and ticks, and you get a second try at anything that did not work. Show that you really know your machine, inside and out.

1. How a Skills Lab Works

1. Typing check first. Do the 3-minute typing check on the typing trainer and write your result below.

2. Four stations, 15 minutes each. Your mentor starts the clock. Read the task and go. When the time is up, stop.

3. The mentor ticks the checklist at the bottom of each station page. Be honest: a tick you did not earn does not help you.

4. Fix and re-try. At the end, go back to any station you did not finish and use its re-try hints.

Station 1: Set Up a Workstation
Session 10 • Page 2
⏱️ TIME LIMIT

15 minutes

🧰 SETUP

System unit, monitor and cable, keyboard, mouse and headset, all unplugged.

🔌 POWER RULE

An adult switches on the power strip. You never touch the wall socket.

1. Your Task

A new computer has arrived at the Hub in pieces. Connect it, start it safely, prove every part works, and shut it down properly.

1
Plan, then connect: match each plug to a port and write it in the table below. Connect the monitor cable (HDMI or VGA, screws finger-tight for VGA), then the keyboard and mouse (USB), then the headset (audio jack or USB). Gently: never force a plug.
2
Start safely: ask the adult to switch on the power strip. Press the monitor's power button, then the system unit's. Sign in with the Hub's practice account.
3
Prove it works: open Notepad and type your name (keyboard), open the Start menu with the mouse, and play a short sound through the headset (choose it as the output device). Sit with the correct posture.
4
Shut down properly: close Notepad without saving, then Start → Power → Shut down. Wait for the lights to go out before the adult switches off the strip.

3
Turn on File name extensions in File Explorer (View → Show). Look at the back and sides of the PC and count its USB ports, and note whether it has HDMI or VGA.

Open Notepad and type the spec sheet using the headings below. Save it as Specs_YourName.txt in Skills_Lab_1.

1. Your Task

Build a tidy portfolio folder, sort the starter files into it, and keep a backup on a pen drive (the two-places rule).

1
In Documents, make a folder called Portfolio_YourName with three subfolders inside: Writing, Pictures and Specs.
2
Right-click Station3_Starter.zip → Extract All → Extract. Inside you will find doc1.txt (a poem) and IMG_0042.png (a picture of the Ghana flag).
3
Rename with F2 using the BIH standard: doc1.txt → English_Poem_YourName.txt, IMG_0042.png → Art_GhanaFlag_YourName.png. Keep the extensions!

Move (cut and paste) the poem into Writing and the picture into Pictures. Copy your Specs_YourName.txt from Station 2 into Specs (not done Station 2 yet? Leave Specs empty and copy it in later).
5
Plug in the pen drive. Copy the whole Portfolio_YourName folder onto it. Open the drive to check all three subfolders arrived. Then eject it safely and wait for "Safe to remove hardware".

🔁 RE-TRY HINTS
• New folder: right-click empty space → New → Folder, type the name, Enter (Session 4).
• Name ends in .txt.txt? Extensions were showing and you typed it twice: F2 and fix it.
• Cut = move (Ctrl + X), copy = twin (Ctrl + C), then Ctrl + V (Session 5).
• Eject: the ^ by the clock → USB icon → Eject, or right-click the drive in This PC → Eject.
