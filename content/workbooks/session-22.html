1. The 3-Minute Showcase Pitch Formula

In tech innovation hubs around the world, entrepreneurs pitch multi-million cedi ideas in just three minutes. You have exactly 180 seconds on stage. Divide your pitch into three tight acts:

Figure 22.0: The 3-Minute Showcase Pitch Arc, followed by 1 minute of audience Q&A. Keep strictly to time so every team has their moment on stage!

2. Showcase Vocabulary

  • Demo Day: An official showcase exhibition where technology students demonstrate working prototypes.
  • The Pitch: A short, persuasive spoken presentation explaining why your project matters and how it works.
  • Live Demonstration: Operating your digital file on the big projector screen in real time before the audience.
  • Call to Action: The final inspiring statement asking people to change a habit, support the project, or adopt the tool.

3. Panic Button / Stage Resilience

🚨 WHAT IF THE PROJECTOR TURNS BLACK ON STAGE?
Stay calm and keep smiling! True tech leaders never freeze.
If a cable comes loose or power flickers, Speaker 1 must keep speaking from memory: describe your project with passion. If needed, Speaker 2 keeps talking while the mentor checks the cable and power. Never touch cables or plugs yourself!
