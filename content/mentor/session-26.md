# Session 26: Skills Lab 3: Office Triathlon

> **At a glance:** a practice-only Skills Lab: pupils do a typing check, then race through four 15-minute stations (an Open Day notice, a club budget with formulas and a chart, a survey data analysis, and a 4-slide deck they present), fix and re-try, and fill in their score sheet. Nothing new is taught. · **Time:** 90 minutes (adjust the timings if your period is shorter) · **Workbook pages:** 6

## Learning outcomes
By the end of the session, pupils can:
1. Turn plain text into a one-page document with a title, headings, a bulleted list and a picture, with no spelling mistakes left, and save it with the right name.
2. Build a spreadsheet that uses SUM, AVERAGE, MAX, MIN, IF and COUNTIF, sort and filter data without breaking rows, and make a correct, titled chart.
3. Make a 4-slide deck that follows the 6-by-6 rule with strong contrast, and present one slide to an audience.

## Key ideas and how to explain them
- **A Skills Lab shows what you can do, not what you can remember.** Pupils may look back at their earlier workbooks during the fix-and-re-try time, but not during the first try. Say: "Real workers look things up too. Today we find out what you can do without looking."
- **Three sports, four stations:** Words (Station 1), Numbers (Stations 2 and 3) and Slides (Station 4). Every station ends with Ctrl + S.
- **Team points:** 1 point for each station a team member finishes inside the time, 1 point when a pupil helps another during fix-and-re-try *by explaining, not by taking the mouse*, and 2 points for the team whose presenter gives the best one-minute talk (the class votes with a show of hands).
- **Honest ticks:** the checklist tells you what to practise next. A missing tick is useful information, not a failure.
- **The planted traps:** spelling mistakes mixed with Ghanaian names (Session 17), the Total row and the chart (Session 20), sorting one column and breaking the rows (Session 22), and a wordy paragraph that breaks the 6-by-6 rule (Session 23).

## The 3D lab
Two labs are open for this session: **Format a Real Document** ("Select paragraphs on a 3D page and use the ribbon: bold, sizes, alignment, bullets, numbering, heading styles, spell check, then print it on a 3D printer") and **3D Spreadsheet Charts** ("Type numbers into a mini spreadsheet and watch columns and pie slices grow, with SUM, AVERAGE, MAX and MIN updating live"). Use them in the fix-and-re-try time: a pupil who struggled at Station 1 does the document lab missions (headings, bullets, spell check, print preview) before trying the notice again; a pupil who struggled at Station 2 does the chart lab missions (click a column to find its cell, change a number and watch SUM change, switch to a pie chart) first. Pupils who finished everything can race a partner through either lab. Do not use the labs instead of a station: every tick needs a real file.

## Common mistakes and quick fixes
| Pupils often… | Try this |
|---|---|
| "Fix" Owusu-Ansah or Kwesi with a wrong suggestion | Ctrl + Z, then Ignore All or Add to Dictionary. Only *computters*, *freind* and *spreadsheats* are real mistakes. |
| Squash the picture with a side handle | Ctrl + Z and drag a corner. In Word, Picture Format → Size keeps the shape too. |
| Save the notice as .txt again (Word may keep the old format) | Use File → Save As and choose Word Document (.docx). |
| Type `SUM(B2:B7)` with no `=` | "What does every formula start with?" |
| Put the IF in C2 but forget to copy it down | Show the AutoFill handle (the small square at the bottom-right of the cell) again. |
| Select A1:B8 for the chart | One giant "Total" bar. Delete the chart, select A1:B7, insert again. |
| Select only column E and sort it | The screen times move but the other columns do not: P07 is no longer the pupil with 6 hours. Ctrl + Z, then click one cell in E and sort. |
| Forget to clear the filter, so COUNTIF looks wrong to them | COUNTIF still counts hidden rows, but pupils get confused. Clear the filter so all 16 rows show. |
| Paste the whole brief paragraph onto one slide | "Would you read this from the back of the hall?" Cut it to 3–4 bullets of 6 words or fewer. |
| Read the slide out word for word | Ask them to look at you and say it in their own words; the slide is the signboard, they are the speaker. |

## When things go wrong
- **No internet:** Word, Excel and PowerPoint (or LibreOffice) work offline, and so do the starter files and both 3D labs from the offline pack. Google-only PCs: pupils pair with a pupil on an offline PC, or wait for the internet and do the paper parts (the storyboard for Station 4) first.
- **Not enough PCs:** use pairs with a **driver and a navigator**. The navigator reads the steps and checks the checklist; the driver does it. Swap roles at every station, so each pupil drives two stations (for example, A drives 1 and 3, B drives 2 and 4). Each pupil then drives their other two stations in the fix-and-re-try time or in the first 20 minutes of Session 27. With very few PCs (one PC for 4 pupils or more), run the stations as a rotation: one group at the PCs on Station 1 while the others do the 3D labs, the Station 4 storyboard on paper and typing practice, then swap every 15 minutes, and finish the remaining stations next session.
- **A PC freezes or the power goes:** this is why every station says save first. Stop the clock for that pupil, restart, and reopen the file from Skills_Lab_3. If the starter file is damaged, copy a fresh one from your USB. If the power goes for everyone, finish on paper: pupils write the formulas for Station 2 and 3 in the workbook and storyboard Station 4, and do the computer parts next time.

## Answer key

**Station 2 (Numbers: budget, Page 3)**
| Cell | Formula | Result |

| B9 | `=AVERAGE(B2:B7)` | **64.67** (64.6666… before rounding) |

**Station 3 (Numbers: survey, Page 4)**
- After freezing, row 1 stays at the top when scrolling (Excel: View → Freeze Panes → Freeze Top Row; Google Sheets: View → Freeze → 1 row; LibreOffice Calc: View → Freeze Cells → Freeze First Row).
- Sorted by screen time, largest to smallest: **P07** (JHS 3, Walk, Kenkey, 6) is on top, then P04 and P13 (5 hours); P03 and P09 (1 hour) are at the bottom.
- Filter Travel = Walk: **8 rows** (P01, P03, P05, P07, P09, P12, P14, P16).
- Labels in G2 to G4, COUNTIF in H2 to H4 (column F left empty, as in Session 22): `=COUNTIF(C2:C17,"Walk")` = **8**; `=COUNTIF(D2:D17,"Jollof")` = **6**; `=COUNTIF(E2:E17,">=4")` = **6** (P02, P04, P07, P10, P13, P15).
- A good finding is true and specific, for example: "Half of the pupils (8 out of 16) walk to school." or "Jollof is the most popular food, chosen by 6 out of 16 pupils." or "6 out of 16 pupils have 4 or more hours of screen time a day."
- Other useful facts if pupils go further: Trotro 4, Bicycle 2, Car 2; Waakye 4, and Banku and tilapia, Fufu and Kenkey 2 each; average screen time 3.125 hours.

**Station 4 (Slides, Page 5):** 4 slides: a Title Slide (title and the pupil's name), then three Title and Content slides, for example "What We Learn" (typing, documents, spreadsheets and charts, surveys, slide shows), "Why Join?" (schoolwork, ready for SHS, skills for jobs, free) with the picture, and "Come Along!" (**Friday 27 November, 2:00 pm, ICT lab**; the club meets Wednesdays 2:30–4:00 pm). No slide has more than 6 bullets or more than 6 words in a bullet; text is dark on light or light on dark and at least 24 pt; the picture is not squashed; the pupil started the show with Shift + F5 (or F5, or the Slideshow button), faced the audience and spoke in their own words for about a minute, then pressed Esc.

**Scoring (Page 6):** 6 ticks per station plus 1 bonus point for finishing inside the time limit, so 7 per station and 28 in total. As a guide: 22–28 strong, 14–21 good progress, below 14 means re-teach the weakest station before Skills Lab 4.

## Support and stretch
- **Needs more support:** give the Club_Budget_Starter.xlsx file so the pupil starts at step 2 of Station 2, and let them do Station 3 steps 1–3 before COUNTIF. Allow their Session 16–24 workbooks open during the re-try. Pair them as navigator first, so they see the steps before driving.
- **Finished early:** in Station 2, add `=IF(B8<=400,"Within budget","Over budget")` in C8, then change the snacks to 90 and watch it change to "Over budget" (total 403). In Station 3, add a column chart of how pupils travel (type the four COUNTIF results in a small table first). In Station 1, add a header with the club name and a page number in the footer. Or help another team by asking questions, not by taking the mouse (team points!).

## Evidence for sign-off
Sign the score sheet when the pupil has a recorded typing check and a saved file for each station they drove, in Skills_Lab_3 with the correct names, and you have initialled each station with the number of ticks. Tick a Passport skill only when you saw it working in the pupil's own file today (for example, click B8 and see `=SUM(B2:B7)`; see P07 on top with its row intact). These ticks are evidence for the **Silver Passport** at Skills Lab 4 (Session 35); nothing is awarded today. Pupils who could not drive all four stations finish them in the next session before their ticks are complete.

**How to re-teach:** for every station where a pupil scored 3 ticks or fewer, write the session number on their score sheet (Station 1: Sessions 16–18; Station 2: Sessions 19–20 and 22; Station 3: Session 22; Station 4: Sessions 23–24) and give them 10 minutes in the next session to redo that station with a fresh starter file, using the re-try hints and their old workbook.

## Safety and wellbeing
Timed races can make some pupils anxious. Remind everyone that this is practice, nobody fails a Skills Lab, and the score is only for them and their mentor; never read individual scores aloud or rank pupils on the board (team points are for teams, not individuals). All data is fictional: the survey uses pupil numbers, not names, which is the "ask only what you need" rule from Session 21. If you use a real photo for Open_Day_Picture.png, it must have no pupils' faces unless you have their consent. Pupils open and save only their own files and ask before deleting anything in the shared folders.
